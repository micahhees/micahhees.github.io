Life Story

Where it starts

Middleton, Wisconsin

Middleton is about 20 minutes from Madison, the capital of Wisconsin. It’s a largely suburban community with great schools, which is why my mom wanted to raise my brother and me here.

High school

Middleton High School

I participated in just about as many clubs as possible. I mainly spent my time on business-competition clubs like FBLA and DECA, but also played JV soccer and tennis (2x most improved player!), and worked some weekdays and weekends at a retirement home, then Target and a local diner.

College decisions

Rejected, then appealed

I initially got rejected from UW–Madison, but sent an appeal and got in!

The world got bigger

First trips abroad

I signed up for an AP Human Geography trip to Southeast Asia after some encouragement from a friend, and worked at Target to make the $800+ monthly payments during my junior year of high school. It ended up being worth every penny.

April 2026

Found AI safety

At an Effective Altruism retreat, which I was also somewhat new to, AI safety was emphasized by nearly every person as a top priority for making a positive impact in the world right now. Since then, I’ve been shifting the focus of nearly all my life decisions to work on this issue.

Recently

Canvassed in NYC

Volunteering for Bores, canvassing 10+ hours a day and meeting incredible people in the process.
