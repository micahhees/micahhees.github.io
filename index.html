About

I study economics at UW–Madison and work on AI safety fieldbuilding: helping more people, especially non-technical people, find their way into work on making AI go well. I’m Growth Coordinator and a fellowship facilitator for Effective Altruism UW–Madison, where I started a summer intro fellowship for incoming students and am working to grow the chapter into a reliable on-ramp to the field. This summer I spent time around Constellation in Berkeley, meeting people in AI safety and helping with whatever projects they had in mind.

Most of my background is in organizing and advocacy. In June I canvassed 10+ hours a day for three weeks for Alex Bores in one of the most expensive House primaries on record, and led a poll site on Election Day. Before that I served as Wisconsin State Chair for American Heart Association advocates, lobbying state legislators, and I’m VP of University Relations at Bridge Madison, which hosts conversations across the political spectrum.

I’m at my best as a connector: getting to know people well and pointing them toward the people and opportunities that fit. Outside of that, I climb, run, and read.
