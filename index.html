Hi, I’m Micah. I study economics at UW–Madison and work on AI safety community building with the Wisconsin AI Safety Initiative, where I run operations, facilitate a technical and a policy fellowship cohort, and help write the curriculum for both.

I think our generation’s greatest challenge will be making AI go well, and that it goes better when more thoughtful people are working on it. So I spend my time finding those people and connecting them with each other, mostly on the people side of AI safety: fieldbuilding, communications, and policy.

Most of my background is in organizing and advocacy. This June I canvassed for Alex Bores in New York and ran a poll site on Election Day. I served as Wisconsin State Chair for American Heart Association advocates, and I’m VP of University Relations at Bridge Madison. I also spent 6+ weeks in Berkeley this summer around AI safety professionals and university organizers.

Outside of work I enjoy running, weightlifting, going out to eat with friends, cooking and baking, and karaoke.
