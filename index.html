01Mission

I study economics at UW–Madison and work on AI safety community building. The transition to advanced AI goes better when more thoughtful people are working on it, technical and not, so I spend my time bringing them in.

At the Wisconsin AI Safety Initiative I’m on the policy team, help build and facilitate our AI Safety Fellowship, and lead recruiting. My roots are in organizing: canvassing in New York, lobbying Wisconsin legislators, and getting people across the political spectrum into the same room.

Off the clock: climbing, running, reading.
